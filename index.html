About Me

Nikhil Komalla (Ph.D. Candidate)

With over seven years deeply immersed in hydrogen energy and computational catalysis, I am a Ph.D. Candidate at The Pennsylvania State University in the Materials & Minerals Theory (MMT) Group (Energy & Mineral Engineering, Minor in Computational Materials). My research bridges quantum-mechanical accuracy and scalable machine learning — uniting spin-polarized DFT, Ab Initio Molecular Dynamics (AIMD), fine-tuned foundation & magnetic MLIPs, and classical MD with MLIPs to accelerate catalyst and materials discovery.

My expertise spans high-throughput surface and interface modeling across transition-metal sulfides, complex columbite oxides, and electrochemical solid–liquid interfaces, integrating explicit microsolvation dynamics, committee uncertainty active learning, and agentic scientific workflows for green energy systems.
